Draw a hand of three pieces, set them on a hex isle, and chase the bonuses each piece earns from its neighbours. Bubbles show exactly what a spot will score before you build.

How it plays

Fill the bar to earn a new pack, pick one of two, and keep building until your pieces run out or the isle is full. Every isle is different, the daily isle is the same for everyone each day, and a bigger weekly isle comes every Monday. An almanac keeps every piece you have met, and feats give you things to aim for.

Realms

The Classic realm is free: twelve pieces, endless isles and the daily isle. Four more realms each bring new pieces and new rules: Red Planet, Corsair Coast, Hallow Vale and Frostfall. Each is a one-time purchase, and All Realms brings all four and every realm still to come.

Made to be calm

Portrait, one-thumb play, on iPhone and iPad, in English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese. Pinch to look closer. No ads, no timers, no energy and no coins. No accounts and no tracking.
