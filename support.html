Common questions

I bought a realm but don't have it.

Open the menu (top left), tap Realms, then Restore at the top right. You'll also find Restore Purchases in the menu under Settings. Realms come back on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

How does the daily isle work?

Everyone gets the same isle each day, from the menu. A new one arrives at 09:00 UTC (5 am Eastern). Play it as often as you like: your best score of the day is kept on your phone, and Your bests in the menu shows your recent days.

Can I see how to play again?

Yes. Open the menu, tap Settings, then How to play for the first isle's short lesson.

Are there ads?

No. Never. There are no timers or coins either.
