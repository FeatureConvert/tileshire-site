Tileshire Privacy Policy

Tileshire · Last updated October 5, 2026

Short version: Tileshire doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Tileshire has no accounts, no ad networks, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no servers of its own, and it makes no network calls of its own. We don't know who plays, and we never see your data.

Your isles and settings

Your isle in progress, your best scores, your almanac and feats, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language) are saved on your device and stay there. The daily isle is made on your device from the date, so nothing is downloaded to make it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The game only learns which realm you bought so it can open it for you, and Restore Purchases asks Apple which realms your Apple Account already owns.

Game Center

Tileshire doesn't use Game Center at the moment. If a future version switches it on, your daily isle scores would be sent to Apple's Game Center for its leaderboard and handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Nothing else would be sent, and we would update this page first.

Children

Tileshire is made for everyone, including families. Because we collect no personal information, we don't collect any from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
